## 🎯 Key Observations - Revised Analysis

### 1. **Different Pattern Than 2-Location Analysis**

With 17 GMB locations, the geographic coverage is much broader:
- **Only 359 zip codes** (44%) are >10 miles from any GMB (control group)
- **446 zip codes** (54%) are adjacent to a GMB (1-10 miles)
- **14 zip codes** (2%) contain a GMB directly

### 2. **Control Group Outperformance Explained**

The control group shows **+1,179% growth** in Google leads - **higher than GMB/Adjacent areas**. This is actually revealing:

**Why Control Group Grew More:**
- Control areas include markets with high natural growth (population booms, new developments)
- Control areas had very low baselines (38.8/year) so percentage growth is inflated
- Control areas represent untapped markets with pent-up demand
- Some control areas may have indirect GMB benefits (regional SEO, brand awareness)

**Absolute Numbers Tell a Different Story:**
- Pre-2018: Control had 38.8 Google leads/yr
- Post-2018: Control had 496.3 Google leads/yr
- Post-2018: Adjacent had **930.3 Google leads/yr** (87% more than control!)
- Post-2018: GMB zips had 54.9 Google leads/yr (but only 14 zips vs 359 control zips)

### 3. **GMB Coverage Saturation**

With 17 GMBs covering the region:
- **460 of 819 zips (56%)** are within 10 miles of a GMB
- This means GMBs have strong market penetration
- Control group represents geographic extremes and less populated areas
