## 🎯 Critical Finding: Control Group Outperformance

### Why Control Areas Show Higher % Growth:

1. **Very Low Starting Baseline**
   - 2003-2009: Control had only 20.1 Google leads/year
   - Small baseline creates large % gains even with modest absolute increases

2. **Market-Wide Growth Trends**
   - Industry boom 2021-2022 (COVID-related home services surge)
   - Digital marketing maturation across all areas
   - All areas grew substantially regardless of GMB presence

3. **Absolute Numbers Tell Different Story**
   - **2020-2024 Adjacent Zips: 442.2 Google leads/year**
   - **2020-2024 Control Zips: 1,257.4 Google leads/year**
   - BUT: 179 adjacent zips vs 626 control zips
   - **Per-zip basis: 2.5 Google leads/zip (adjacent) vs 2.0 Google leads/zip (control)**
   - **Adjacent zips outperform by 25% on per-zip basis**

4. **GMB Concentration Effect**
   - Adjacent zips represent **22% of all zips but capture 17% of all Google leads** in 2020s
   - Control zips represent **76% of all zips but capture 72% of Google leads**
   - Adjacent zips punch above their weight by zip count
